# Client Profiles Documentation

_Last Modified: 2024-05-18_  
_Version: 1.0.0_

## Overview

This document details the comprehensive client profile structure designed to support Group Training Organizations (GTOs), Labour Hire operations, and compliance with Australian Industrial Relations laws. The client profile system accommodates the complex relationships between host employers, apprentices/trainees, and the GTO/Labour Hire provider.

## Client Profile Types

### 1. Host Employer Profiles

Host employer profiles are designed for businesses that engage apprentices, trainees, or labour hire workers through your organization.

#### 1.1 Core Information

- Business name and ABN/ACN
- Trading name(s)
- Business structure (Sole trader, Partnership, Company, Trust)
- Industry classification (ANZSIC code)
- Business size (number of employees)
- Physical address(es)
- Postal address
- Contact details (primary, secondary, emergency)
- Operating hours

#### 1.2 Compliance Information

- WorkCover/Workers Compensation details
  - Policy number
  - Expiry date
  - Coverage details
- Public liability insurance
  - Policy number
  - Coverage amount
  - Expiry date
- Professional indemnity insurance (if applicable)
  - Policy number
  - Coverage amount
  - Expiry date
- Work Health & Safety assessment status
  - Last assessment date
  - Risk rating
  - Required improvements
  - Follow-up schedule

#### 1.3 Agreement Details

- Agreement type
- Start date
- End date (if applicable)
- Renewal terms
- Service level agreements
- Charge rate structures
  - Base rates
  - Penalty rates
  - Allowances
  - Administration fees
- Payment terms
- Special conditions

#### 1.4 Host Capacity

- Maximum apprentice/trainee capacity
- Current placements
- Supervisor details
  - Name
  - Position
  - Qualifications
  - Contact details
  - Supervision ratio compliance
- Available trades/skills
- Rotation capabilities

#### 1.5 Compliance History

- Site visits log
  - Visit date
  - Conducted by
  - Findings
  - Actions required
  - Follow-up status
- Incident reports
  - Date and nature of incident
  - Involved parties
  - Resolution status
  - Preventative measures implemented
- Audit history
  - Audit date
  - Audit type
  - Findings
  - Corrective actions
  - Compliance status

### 2. Enterprise Clients

Enterprise clients are larger organizations that may have multiple locations and complex workforce requirements.

#### 2.1 Additional Enterprise Features

- Corporate structure
- Multiple location management
- Centralized billing options
- Volume pricing structures
- Key account manager assignment
- Service level agreement metrics
- Corporate reporting requirements
- Integration capabilities with client HRIS/ERP systems

### 3. Government/Statutory Clients

Specialized profile type for government departments and statutory bodies.

#### 3.1 Government-Specific Details

- Procurement framework details
- Panel arrangement information
- Contract/tender reference numbers
- Reporting requirements
- Security clearance requirements
- Indigenous procurement policy compliance
- Social procurement framework alignment
