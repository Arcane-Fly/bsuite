# Functional Requirements

_Last Modified: 2024-02-20_  
_Version: 1.0.0_

## 1. Apprentice Management

### 1.1 Apprentice Profiles

- System must maintain comprehensive apprentice records including:
  - Personal information (name, contact details, emergency contacts)
  - Employment details (start date, employment type, status)
  - Training contract information
  - Qualification details
  - Host placement history
  - Compliance records
  - Performance history

### 1.2 Training Contract Management

- System must track:
  - Contract registration numbers
  - Start and expected completion dates
  - Probation periods
  - Training plan status
  - RTO details and progress
  - Variations and extensions

### 1.3 Progress Tracking

- Monitor and record:
  - Off-the-job training attendance
  - Unit completion status
  - Competency achievements
  - Performance reviews
  - Support provided

## 2. Host Employer Management

### 2.1 Host Profiles

- Maintain records of:
  - Company details
  - Contact persons
  - Agreement terms
  - Placement history
  - Safety assessments
  - Financial arrangements

### 2.2 Placement Management

- Support:
  - Apprentice-host matching
  - Placement creation and modification
  - Rotation planning
  - Host capacity tracking
  - Charge rate management

## 3. Compliance Management

### 3.1 Document Management

- Track and store:
  - Training contracts
  - Training plans
  - Host agreements
  - Induction records
  - Safety documentation
  - Licenses and certifications

### 3.2 Monitoring and Alerts

- Automated alerts for:
  - Expiring documents
  - Due compliance checks
  - Required site visits
  - Training deadlines
  - License renewals

## 4. Financial Management

### 4.1 Timesheet Processing

- Support:
  - Online timesheet submission
  - Host approval workflow
  - Training time tracking
  - Leave management
  - Overtime calculation

### 4.2 Payroll Integration

- Enable:
  - Award interpretation
  - Pay rate progression
  - Allowance calculation
  - Leave accrual
  - Superannuation tracking

### 4.3 Host Billing

- Manage:
  - Charge rate calculation
  - Invoice generation
  - Payment tracking
  - Credit management
  - Financial reporting

### 4.4 Government Funding

- Track and process:
  - Incentive eligibility
  - Claim submissions
  - Payment reconciliation
  - Audit documentation

## 5. Reporting and Analytics

### 5.1 Operational Reports

- Generate reports on:
  - Apprentice statistics
  - Completion rates
  - Host performance
  - Compliance status
  - Financial metrics

### 5.2 Compliance Reports

- Produce:
  - Regulatory submissions
  - Audit documentation
  - Training progress reports
  - Safety statistics
  - Funding acquittals

## 6. User Access and Communication

### 6.1 Role-Based Access

- Implement access levels for:
  - GTO staff
  - Host employers
  - Apprentices
  - RTOs
  - Auditors

### 6.2 Communication

- Enable:
  - Automated notifications
  - Document sharing
  - Message tracking
  - Broadcast announcements
  - Feedback collection
