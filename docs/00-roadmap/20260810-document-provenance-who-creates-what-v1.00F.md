# Who creates each document — and what that changes

**Operator ruling, 2026-08-10.** Recorded, then applied to all 83 document
categories in the system.

> "Training contracts is provided by the STA and is uploaded.
> Host agreement, or charge terms or similar generated by the GTO in the system
> and issued for our esigning.
> Apprentices trainees and workers will have employment contracts. Similar
> generated by the GTO in the system and issued for our esigning."

---

## 1. The rule this establishes

**Provenance — who authored the artefact — is the property that decides how a
document behaves.** Not its subject, not its folder, not what it is called.

Three origins, and the third is one the ruling implies rather than names:

| Origin | Who authored it | What the platform must do |
|---|---|---|
| **Uploaded** | An external authority — STA, ASQA, a government agency, an RTO, an insurer, a doctor, the worker | Accept a file, record what it is, **verify it**, track expiry |
| **Generated** | The GTO, in CRM7, from its own data | Hold a **template**, render it, **issue it for e-signature**, keep the executed copy |
| **Captured** | Nobody — it is structured data a person typed | Store fields, not a file. It should not be a "document" at all |

Everything downstream follows from which of the three a category is: whether it
needs a template, an upload control, a verification step, an expiry date, a
signature, or none of them.

---

## 2. What exists today

**Signing is built and works.** `charge_rate_quote_signing_tokens` holds a live
token, and the quote signing path — issue, open, sign, redeem — runs end to end.
`signature_requests` has 5 rows. There is a `SignatureCanvas`, a `TripleSignOff`
component, a `SendForSignatureDialog`, and two signature pages.

**Generation is built four times, for four specific things.** The NSGTO Standard
2 pack, the portable long-service-leave PDF, the report-template runner, and
`documentSigner.ts`. Each solves its own case.

**`document_templates` has 0 rows.** The general path — a GTO authors a template
once and the platform renders it per person, per host, per placement — is the
gap. Every generated document today is a bespoke renderer.

## 3. All 83 categories, classified

### UPLOADED — an external authority created it (36)

The platform's job is to accept, classify, verify and expire. **It must never
pretend it can produce these.**

- **Identity & Work Rights** (4) — driver's licence, passport, birth certificate,
  visa. Government-issued.
- **Qualifications & Licences** (5) — trade qualification, white card, forklift,
  first aid, HR/HC/MC licence. RTO or authority-issued, all with expiry.
- **Compliance & Safety** (5) — police check, WWCC, pre-employment medical, drug
  and alcohol result, immunisation. Third-party issued, all expiring.
- **GTO Compliance, the regulator half** (3) — ASQA registration, State Training
  Authority document, scope of registration.
- **Insurance** (3 + certificate of currency) — insurer-issued.
- **The training contract** — **STA-provided, per the ruling.**
- **Candidate-supplied** — resume, cover letter, reference.
- **WHS third-party** — medical report, workers compensation claim.

### GENERATED — the GTO authors it in CRM7, then it is e-signed (31)

- **Host Employer Agreement** — named in the ruling.
- **Charge terms / rate card** — named in the ruling. **Already has a working
  signing path**; this is the model for the rest.
- **Employment Contract** — apprentice, trainee and worker. Named in the ruling.
- Position Description, Site Risk Assessment, Host Employer Application.
- **Field Officer Reports** (7) — workplace visit, site assessment, placement
  monitoring, progress review, mentoring notes, intervention plan, competency
  sign-off. Every one is authored in-system.
- **WHS authored** (6) — incident report, witness statement, investigation
  report, risk assessment, hazard report, return-to-work plan.
- **Quality Assurance** (4) — corrective action, non-conformance, complaint
  register, assessment validation.
- **GTO-authored compliance** (5) — policy/procedure, compliance audit report,
  quality assurance record, risk management document, complaints and appeals.
- **Funding and finance** — invoice, receipt, payment confirmation.
- **Legal & Governance** (5) — board minutes, director declaration, service
  agreement, regulatory submission.

Not all of these need a signature. **Generated and signed are different
properties** — an incident report is generated and not signed; a host agreement
is both.

### CAPTURED — this is data, not a document (the finding worth acting on)

**Bank Account Details** and **Superannuation Choice Form** are listed as
document categories. They are also, since 2026-08-10, structured columns written
by `save_person_bank_account` and `save_person_tax_declaration`, with the account
number in the encrypted vault and every decryption logged.

**So the same fact has two homes.** A worker can satisfy "bank details" by
uploading a photo of a bank statement, or by typing an account number into the
payroll vault, and the platform will not agree with itself about whether the
requirement is met. **Tax File Number Declaration** has the same split.

That is the classic duplication this estate keeps paying for, and it is cheap to
fix now because the onboarding requirements registry is new.

## 4. Recommendation

### 4.1 Make provenance a column, not a convention

Add to `document_categories`:

```
origin              uploaded | generated | captured
requires_signature  boolean
issued_to           worker | host | internal | regulator
```

**One field then answers every question the UI keeps asking badly.** Whether to
show an upload control or a "Generate" button. Whether the portal should ask the
worker for it at all — a worker cannot upload a host agreement, and asking them
to is the kind of nonsense the onboarding checklist will otherwise produce.
Whether a verification step applies. Whether it needs an expiry.

This is one migration and it is the highest-leverage thing on this page.

### 4.2 Build the generate → sign path ONCE, and copy the quote

**Do not build a document generator per document type.** There are 31 generated
categories; four bespoke renderers already exist and none of them share anything.

The charge-rate quote already does the whole job: it is generated from system
data, issued to an external party who has no login, signed through a
single-use expiring token, and the executed copy is kept as evidence. That path
is live, and it is the pattern.

Generalise it: `document_templates` (currently empty) holds the template,
the existing signing token mechanism carries it, and each new document type
becomes a template plus a data binding rather than a new renderer.

**Order:** Host Employer Agreement first — it is named in the ruling, it is the
GTO's core commercial instrument, and `host_agreements` already exists with zero
rows waiting for it. Employment contracts second, because they need the
employment-type and award context that W2 and R8 have only just made reliable.

### 4.3 Stop treating captured data as documents

Bank details, superannuation choice and the TFN declaration should be **captured
fields with an optional supporting document**, not document categories in their
own right. The requirements registry should ask "are these fields complete",
not "has a file been uploaded".

An uploaded bank statement is *evidence for* the captured account number. It is
not the account number.

### 4.4 Never let the platform imply it can produce an uploaded document

For all 36 uploaded categories the only actions are request, accept, verify and
expire. **The training contract is the one to get right first**, because it is
the STA's instrument and a GTO that appears to generate its own training
contracts is a compliance problem, not a UX one.

---

## 5. What this does not settle

- **Whether the GTO counter-signs an uploaded training contract.** The STA
  issues it; whether CRM7 records the GTO's acknowledgement against it is a
  separate question and I have not assumed an answer.
- **Who signs an employment contract on the GTO side**, and whether that
  authority is a role in the platform or a named person.
- **Retention.** Uploaded regulator documents and executed agreements almost
  certainly have different statutory retention periods. Nothing in the platform
  models retention today.
- The **four contract tables** (crm7#1577) — this ruling makes the answer
  clearer, since `training_contracts` is uploaded and `host_agreements` is
  generated, which are genuinely different lifecycles. But it does not settle
  what `contracts` and `host_contracts` are for.
