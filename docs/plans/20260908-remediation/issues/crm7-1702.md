## Related prior work (not a duplicate)
crm7#1279 ("Change of Year rollover page has no way to create the first batch — persist logic is real but bootstrap is missing", closed COMPLETED 2026-07-30) fixed a narrower gap: the existing `/admin/change-of-year` page had real persist logic but no way to create the first batch. This item is broader — it covers the wage-anniversary REMINDER and NOTICE capability (to both apprentice and host), which #1279 does not address.

## Cross-reference: crm7 half of D-68
Directive D-68 (filed in parallel in GaryOcean428/R80.4) rules that competency-based progression, anniversary reminders, and change-of-year notices belong in crm7, not in the R80.4 rate calculator — R80.4's calculation is point-in-time (what an apprentice is worth NOW), and the ongoing records/reminders/notices tracking this item covers are the crm7-side counterpart of that ruling. Search R80.4 issues for "competency-based progression" / "D-68" to find the calculator-side ruling issue.

## Acceptance criteria
- Wage-anniversary dates are tracked per apprentice (derived from their training contract), independent of any competency-based progression logic.
- A reminder mechanism fires ahead of each wage-anniversary / change-of-year date.
- A notice capability exists that can notify both the apprentice and the host employer of an upcoming or completed change-of-year event.

## Mandatory before merge
- **Validation loop:** §9.1 output-equivalence — reminders/notices must fire based on real anniversary dates derived from the training contract, not a manually re-entered date.
- **Equivalence target:** A reminder/notice generated by this feature reflects the same anniversary date as the underlying training contract record — no drift between the two.
