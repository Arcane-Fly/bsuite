# No way to enter funding amount, payment timeframes, or how funding is applied (passthrough/offset/wage top-up/custom)

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/crm7/issues/1691

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-09-06T09:16:05Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

Operator observed: No way to enter the funding amount, payment timeframes and dates, or how it is applied — passthrough to host, offset against rates, passthrough to apprentice, wage top-up, or custom priority categories.

**Route/surface:** funding record entry (wherever a funding source is attached to an engagement/apprentice)

Directive: D-80 (2026-08-13)

## Acceptance criteria
- Funding entry supports: amount, payment timeframe/dates (start, end, milestone dates), and an explicit "how applied" category selector covering at minimum: passthrough to host, offset against charge rates, passthrough to apprentice, wage top-up, and a custom/priority category option.
- Each "how applied" category has the field shape it actually needs (e.g. a rate offset needs a %/$ and a rate reference; a passthrough needs a recipient).
- Per D-69 (companion directive in R80.4 for the calculator side): funding milestone fields must not be pre-populated — empty with placeholder text naming the field, so this crm7-side entry form should follow the same non-pre-populated convention.

## Mandatory before merge
- **Validation loop:** §9.1 output-equivalence — entered funding data must be retrievable/usable by downstream consumers (rate calc, reporting) in the shape the category implies.
- **Equivalence target:** A funding record entered here produces the same usable shape as the categories require in R80.4's calculation and in crm7's funding reporting.
- **Cross red-team:** bsuite-user-advocate
- **Skills to load:** biz-au-apprenticeship, general-dry-one-shot-architecture
