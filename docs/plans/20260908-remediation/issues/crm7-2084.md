# Dashboard: eleven measured layout defects, all live in production — two nav systems at once, a FAB that eats a link, and an inert card

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/crm7/issues/2084

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-08-26T19:38:29Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

Found by the ruling V-1 visual gate while inspecting `/dashboard` for a promotion. **All eleven are pre-existing and every one was re-confirmed against production** (`crm.crm7.app/dashboard`, same account) — none were introduced by the change being gated. Filing rather than bundling, per ruling V-2.

Measured across 8 cells: light and dark × 1440 / 1024 / 768 / 390.

## The ones that cost a user something

**1. Two complete navigation systems render simultaneously (768–1023px).**
A persistent 255×1024 sidebar *and* a fixed mobile tab bar (`fixed bottom-0 ... z-45 lg:hidden`). `lg:hidden` hides the bar only at ≥1024, but the sidebar begins at 768 — so the whole iPad-portrait band gets both. Confirmed absent at 390 and 1024.

**2. That fixed bar occludes content, and the footer reserves no space for it.**
- At 768: bottom 56px of the footer hidden — copyright cut mid-sentence at "Relationship Management." Sidebar entries clipped: "R8 Calculator" half-cut, **"Conduit ATS" fully hidden**.
- At 390: the footer link **"A Braden Group Company" is entirely behind the bar and unreachable.**

**3. The "Open Jodie" FAB overlaps a footer link at every desktop width — and will intercept its clicks.**
`div.fixed bottom-4 right-4 z-50`, 56×62. At 1440 it covers 40px of the 167px-wide "A Braden Group Company" link, rendering it "A Braden Group Con". At `z-50` over a link, the click lands on the FAB. Same at 1024 and 768.

**4. "Pipeline Overview" is inert.**
The only one of seven grid cards with **zero interactive descendants** — 0 links, 0 buttons. It shows Pipeline Value, Deals Won, Active Accounts, Leads Generated and offers no route to the pipeline, on a page whose nav has a "Sales Pipeline" entry. Its six siblings carry 1–3 links each; the KPI strip above it is properly wired. Acting on any number in that card means leaving the page by hand.

## Layout failures in the 768 band

**5. Footer wordmark overprints the copyright** — right edge 354 vs left edge 346, an **8px negative gap**, legible as "CRM[7]Customer". Glued at 1024 (+2px), fine at 1440 (270px).

**6. All four KPI labels ellipsised at 768** — the label box is pinned to 94px while the icon takes the rest: "Active Opportunities" is 140px into 94px, cut 33% to "Active Oppo…". Clean at 1440/1024/390.

## Density

**7. At 390 the scrollable well is 212px of an 844px viewport** — 2822px of dashboard read through a 212px slot. Cause is a permanently pinned 273px footer *outside* the scroller plus the 56px tab bar. On production without the preview banner it is 410px — still under half the screen, so **the pinned footer is the structural cause, not the preview banner.**

**8. 392px of empty canvas below the last card at 1440** — 25% of scrollable content blank. Not grid slack: `.react-grid-layout` ends 6px after the last card; the empty run belongs to the canvas wrapper beneath it. 317px at 1024.

## What was NOT evaluated

**V-C6 (canvas columns) is UNKNOWN, not passing.** The columns control is not in the resting DOM, and interaction was forbidden because the inspection ran as a real user account — `user_preferences` is keyed by user and shared across environments, so a drag would have permanently rewritten that operator's real saved layout. Being re-run as the seeded CI identity, where interaction is safe.

## What passed

V-C1 (pure endpoints), V-C3, V-C4, V-C5, V-C7, V-C8, V-C9 clean on all 8 cells. Theme signals agreed everywhere — `class` and `data-theme` matched on every cell, no divergence. Body ground `lab(95.476)` light / `lab(3.998)` dark, neither a pure endpoint.

V-C10 passed **on presence of accessible names only** — that does not establish anything is keyboard-reachable, and should not be read as if it did.
