# Switching tabs mid-edit silently discards on-costs and employee-numbering drafts

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/crm7/issues/2458

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-09-05T14:28:41Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

Surfaced while correcting a comment in #2448, and it is worth its own ticket because the unsaved-work registry cannot help here.

## What a user loses

Start editing the on-costs section, or employee numbering, under settings configuration. Switch to another tab before saving. Switch back. The field is empty and the edit is gone. Nothing warned, and nothing can be recovered.

## Why the usual protection does not apply

Both cards sit inside a `TabsContent`, and there is no `forceMount` anywhere in the file, so radix unmounts them when the tab changes. The component holding the draft is the thing being destroyed.

That is why this cannot be closed by declaring the work to the unsaved-work registry. A registration protects a draft that still exists — it arms a prompt before a reload or an organisation switch. Here the draft is already gone by the time anything could ask, and the action that destroyed it is an ordinary in-page tab click that no guard intercepts.

## Not the same as the four page-level forms

Four other forms on this page are declared at page level, so their form objects outlive the tab. For those, switching away and back really does show the edit, and treating them as retained work is correct. Only these two are positioned inside the tab content in a way that unmounts them.

That distinction is the whole point of filing this separately. A uniform fix across the page would be wrong in one direction or the other, which is exactly how the original mistaken comment arose.

## Fix direction

Either `forceMount` on the two tab panels so they keep their state across a tab change, or lift their state above the tab boundary the way the other four already are. Lifting is the more consistent answer, because it makes all six forms on the page behave the same way and removes the special case rather than papering over it.

This is a change to the page's shape, not to the unsaved-work class.

## Acceptance

Type into on-costs, switch tabs, switch back, and the text is still there. Same for employee numbering. Then confirm the unsaved-work prompt still fires on a reload with that edit pending, so the fix does not achieve retention by dropping the registration.

A test must fail against the current markup. Asserting the field is empty after a tab switch passes today and encodes the defect.
