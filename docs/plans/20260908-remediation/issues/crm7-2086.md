# Visual gate: a pure-white minimap on dark, two-thirds of the pipeline off-canvas on mobile, and a columns control that does nothing

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/crm7/issues/2086

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-08-27T01:02:37Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

Measured on `d.crm.crm7.app` as `e2e@crm7.app` (address echoed into evidence per the skill), 8 cells per route — light and dark × 1440/1024/768/390. **Every value below was re-confirmed against production**, so all of these are pre-existing and none were introduced by the promotion being gated. Filed separately per ruling V-2.

Companion to #2084, which covers `/dashboard`.

---

## `/sales/pipeline-flow` — BLOCK

**1. The minimap paints pure white on dark — L = 1.0000 exactly.**
`.react-flow__minimap` renders `rgb(255,255,255)`, 200×150, in **both themes**. On the dark canvas that is a **19.25:1 white slab**. This is exactly the V-C1 class the probe exists to catch: a screenshot reads it as "a light minimap", and only compositing to OKLCH shows it hitting the pure endpoint.

**2. Two-thirds of the pipeline is off-canvas below 1024px.**
At 768 and 390, **only 2 of 6 stages sit inside the canvas rect**. `fitView` clamps at React Flow's default `minZoom` 0.5, so nodes render at 90×34 and the rest simply fall outside. The page promises a "visual deal-stage graph"; on a phone it shows a third of one. Raising `minZoom` (or fitting with padding on a smaller floor) is the fix.

**3. Zoom controls are effectively invisible in dark — 1.19:1.**
Zoom In / Zoom Out / Fit View icons measure **1.19:1** against their own button. The icon inherits `lab(93.001)` from crm7's dark text token onto a light control surface. WCAG 1.4.11 needs 3:1 for controls.

*Note:* V-C6/C7/C8 are structurally **N/A** here — this is a full-bleed React Flow route with 0 cards and no `.react-grid-layout` root, so those classes have zero subjects. Recorded as not-applicable rather than passed.

## `/settings/users` — BLOCK

**4. The columns control is INERT.** UNKNOWN closed as **FAIL**, not passed. The documented 3-click path was run: the dialog opens, `page-editor-edit` clicks cleanly — and then nothing. **Before/after DOM identical.** A control that updates state but never the layout is cosmetic, and cosmetic is a FAIL.

**5. "Remove" button fails contrast in dark — 3.84:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement**, `lab(48.9 73.0 48.2)` on `rgb(15,20,29)` at 12px, twice per cell. Identical on production.

**6. The Subscription Plans tab has zero controls of its own.** It states billing "remains centralized in Business Suite" and **links nowhere**. Its sibling tabs all carry working controls. A dead end with a signpost and no road.

## `/settings/schema-builder` — BLOCK

**7. Dark-mode elevation is a no-op on all 45 entity cards.** `box-shadow` resolves to ambient ink only — `oklch(0.080 0.0200)`, chroma **0.0166**, below the 0.05 glow floor. The cards read flat against the canvas in dark.

**8. At 390 the canvas is 390×582 with 26% of it permanently covered** — the dismissible quick-start toast takes 16% and the fixed bottom nav another 10%.

## `/people` — REVIEW

**9. Three distinct card shadows across 11 cards** in light mode — 6 flat, 4 with `lab(44.8 21.0 -75.5/0.1) 0 1px 3px`, 1 a second flat variant. Dark is clean. Same surface, three elevations.

**10. `color-scheme` is `normal` in dark** on the root and all three real scroll containers, so **UA scrollbars paint light on a dark page**.
