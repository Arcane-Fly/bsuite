# No working save-template / save-as-default / export-to-PDF / push-to-record / eSign flow for quotes, contracts, or documents

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/R80.4/issues/231

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-09-06T09:14:12Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

Five findings across R80.4 and crm7, one root cause: **there is no working path to take a quote or contract out of the calculator/editor and into a usable, signable, or storable artifact anywhere in the suite.**

## What a user cannot do

- **Export to PDF, email for eSigning, or push to an apprentice/host record** from the R8 calculator — none of these work. Also: no way to import a template/quote back from R8. Operator, verbatim (repeat_offence=true): *"Still no way to export to pdf, email for esigning, or push to an apprentice or host record."* (note.123)
- **"Save template" and "save as default" don't work.** "Save as default" doesn't even persist across a page refresh (note.121).
- **"R8 can't save and can't save a template"** — restated independently, repeat_offence=true (note.125). This is the same defect as note.121, reported twice on the same pass.
- **Training contract e-signatures page doesn't make sense as built.** Training contracts are created by the State Training Authority as scanned PDFs — e-signing needs to take the STA's clean PDF and layer a customizable signature block for all parties, not treat this as a from-scratch document. The same requirement applies to host contracts, employment contracts, WHS risk assessments, site-visit attendance, quotes, and rate/charge confirmations. Operator: *"This page makes no sense... this needs to be thought through rather than being so limited and even now unfunctional for its semi miss intended purpose."* (note.131)

## URLs tested

- `crm.crm7.app` (R80.4 charge calculator) — export/save/template
- `crm.crm7.app/contracts/training/e-signatures`

## Done means

One export/save-template/push-to-record/eSign pipeline exists and is reused everywhere a quote or contract needs to leave the app — not five separate half-built implementations. Concretely:
- A quote in R8 can be saved as a reusable template, saved as the calculator's default, exported to PDF, and pushed to the linked apprentice/host record — each verified end to end, not just as a UI state flip.
- The eSigning flow accepts an externally-produced clean PDF (e.g. the STA's scanned training contract) and layers a signature block, rather than assuming the document originates inside the app.
- The same signing primitive is reused for host contracts, employment contracts, WHS risk assessments, site-visit attendance, quotes, and rate/charge confirmations — named individually as consuming surfaces at closure.
