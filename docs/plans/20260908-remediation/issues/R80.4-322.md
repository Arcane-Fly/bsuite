# The 'Apprentice % of Standard Rate' card renders its title and none of its body — the Adult/Junior/School-based cohort hierarchy is unreachable on production despite shipping in the bundle

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/R80.4/issues/322

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-09-07T12:31:29Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

## The "Apprentice % of Standard Rate" card renders its title and none of its body

On production `r8.crm7.app` at commit `10187e2` (built 2026-09-06T04:56:25Z — the commit the
parent gitlink points at, so this is current), the card's header renders and **`PctWagesPanel`'s
entire contents do not**. Nothing errors.

The user-visible consequence is the whole of operator note **D-11**: the
Junior / Adult (21+) / School-based cohort hierarchy the operator asked for is **unreachable**.
The only cohort control on the page is a flat pair — `Did not complete Yr 12` /
`Completed Yr 12` — sitting under the label *"Pick the cohort"*, which is a different card
(`wages`) and a different question.

(`/auth/login?return_path=%2F`; `bs_access_token` present, so no 401), viewport 1800×1100,
award `MA000020` selected, `Apprenticeship` engagement, `Load MA000020 rates` clicked.

Every one of those `panelMarkers` is a string from inside `PctWagesPanel`. All absent.

**The cohort trio is unconditional inside that panel** (`charge-calculator-v9-2.tsx:2206-2218` —
a bare `<div className="mb-2.5">`, no guard). So its absence is not a conditional not firing; the
panel body is not being rendered at all.

## It is not a build problem — the code is in the shipped chunk

This is the check that turns "not rendered" into a real finding, and my first attempt at it was
wrong: I grepped `index-DVSIO93W.js` and got zero for everything, **including strings I could see
on screen**. That zero was my instrument failing, not evidence. The app is code-split and the
calculator lives in a lazy chunk.

Against the chunk the page actually loads,
`/assets/charge-calculator-v9-2-CHeTmVjz.js` (879,782 bytes):

```
POSITIVE CONTROLS (strings visibly rendering on the page)
  "Apprentice % of Standard Rate"   3
  "Did not complete Yr 12"          1
  "Pick the cohort"                 1
THE QUESTION
  "Adult (21+)"                     1     <- SHIPPED
  "School-based"                    2
  "Apprenticeship stream"           1
  "greater of the two"              2
  "Lowest classification"           1
  "No verified percentage table"    1
```

So: shipped in the bundle, card registered in `DASH_DEFAULT_ORDER` (`:901`) with a default span
of 2 (`:937`), title renders, zero errors — and no DOM.

## I have not identified the mechanism and am not guessing

What I can rule out: it is not a missing build, not a stale deployment, not a saved layout
hiding the card, not a thrown error, not a conditional inside the panel, and not an
unauthenticated 401 (the authed run has a token and no 401). Other cards' bodies render normally
on the same page — `wages`, `trade`, `fairwork`, `employment` all show their contents — so it is
specific to this card.

Worth noting for whoever picks it up: `document.querySelectorAll('.react-grid-item').length` is
**0** on this page, so whatever renders these cards is not react-grid-layout here, and the card
chrome ("Hover a card for its ⠿ drag and ⤢ resize grips") is present regardless. That is where I
would look first — how a card's body gets mounted, versus how its header does.

## Why this matters more than a missing control

Operator note D-11's recorded evidence says *"The cited controls DO exist exactly where
claimed — I confirmed cohort buttons at charge-calculator-v9-2.tsx:2155-2168"*. That was a
**source** confirmation and it is true of the file. It is false of the page. Anyone reading that
row would reasonably conclude the control is on screen and merely wired to the wrong number;
the measurement says a user cannot select Adult at all.

D-11's other findings are unaffected and still hold in source: the wage auto-fill key
(`:6064`) omits `cohortMode` entirely and selects only between `awardMinWages.y12` and
`.no12` (`:6068`), `adultFloor` has exactly one call site (`:6148`) whose `adultRows` result is
consumed only at `:7658` for rendering, and `cohortMode` reaches the payroll-tax rate type
(`:5921`) and allowance treatment (`:5945`) but never the wage. So even once the panel renders,
selecting Adult will still not move the pay rate — that is a second, separate fix.

## Acceptance criteria

1. On a **fresh browser context** on `r8.crm7.app`, with an award selected and Apprenticeship
   engagement, the cohort trio is in the DOM and on screen. Test fresh — a saved layout or a
   warm session is capable of hiding this.
2. A regression test that asserts the rendered output of the `pctwages` card, not the presence
   of the string in the source or the bundle. Both of those are currently true and neither
   caught this.
3. Separately (D-11's own finding): selecting Adult changes the wage the quote is struck from,
   with a planted control — an adult apprentice's wage must differ from the junior figure where
   `adultFloor` says it should.
