# [BACKLOG — not a priority] ABN contractor engagement (no super / WC / payroll tax / leave loading)

https://github.com/GaryOcean428/R80.4/issues/5

Snapshot updatedAt: 2026-08-24T03:26:34Z. Open at capture; re-read live.

**Operator direction 2026-08-08: "eventually but it's not a priority."**

Parked deliberately so it is not lost and not worked. Do not pick this up ahead of anything else.

## What it is

R80.4 models **Apprentice / Trainee / Worker** × FT / PT / Casual, with engagement-aware payroll-tax resolution (`src/awards/payroll-tax.ts:109-133` correctly refuses a training-contract code for `worker`). "Worker + Casual" covers labour-hire and qualified casuals.

There is **no ABN contractor engagement**. `grep -in "contractor\|\babn\b"` across R80.4 → zero hits.

`calculate()` (`src/awards/calculate.ts:25-571`) applies superannuation and workers' comp to every engagement/employment-type combination it models. Nothing branches on "this person is not an employee."

## What would be needed if it is ever picked up

An engagement that suppresses the on-costs that only attach to employment:
- superannuation
- workers' compensation
- payroll tax
- leave loading and leave accrual

## Open scope question — answer before building, not during

Whether an ABN contractor belongs in the **rate engine** at all, or only in crm7/invoicing. A contractor invoices; they are not paid a wage against an award. If there is no award interpretation to do, the engine may be the wrong home and this may be purely a charge/invoice concern.

Per **RULING 9.1** — *"Rates are entered by the user and saved. The calculator does arithmetic."* — that question probably resolves toward crm7. Confirm with the operator before any code.

## Provenance

Residual from `GaryOcean428/R80.3#388`, which was closed as **FIXED** — the titled defect (employment-type selector hardcoded to apprentice+trainee) is resolved. This was the only part of that issue's acceptance criteria not met, and it is a new requirement rather than the original bug.

## Not in scope
Do not add an `'ABN'` value to any existing enum or `IN (...)` list as a placeholder. See crm7#1473 for why that pattern is the recurring defect.
